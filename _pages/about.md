I am a linguist with a focus on syntax. In my research, I conduct cross-linguistic experiments on ellipsis. My main focus lies on English and German, but I have also conducted experiments in Dutch and Icelandic.

Currently, I work in the CLEFs project at the University of Tübingen, supervised by Prof. Dr. James Griffiths.
